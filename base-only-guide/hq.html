HQ tab

Approval list · Approve / Reject · Warehouse handoff

For HQ / approvers. Review pending submissions, Approve or Reject, then fill details so Warehouse PIC can ship.

Who uses this tab?

Approvers who own the go / no-go decision and prepare Part 1 details for Warehouse.

You do here

  • Review the approval list
  • Check products + creator
  • Approve or Reject
  • Fill qty / variation / shipping for Warehouse PIC

What Approve triggers

  • Flow Status → Approved
  • Warehouse Processing rows created (one per product)
  • Team notified in chat

How to review & decide

  1. Open the HQ tab.
  2. Filter by approval stage: Pending Approval, Approved, or Rejected.
  3. Open a submission from the list. Check Selected Products, creator info, and notes.
  4. Decide:
    • Approve — only when products are linked and details look correct.
    • Reject — add a reject reason when needed; team gets notified.
  5. After Approve, fill HQ Part 1 fields for Warehouse PIC (qty, size/colour/variation, shipping address, phone, notes).
  6. Track handoff with warehouse filters: Pending HQ Fill → Pending Warehouse → Shipped → Done.
Remember: Approve is what creates Warehouse rows. If Selected Products is empty, fix the links first — don’t approve yet.

Before you Approve checklist

Must check

  • Selected Products linked
  • Creator(s) look correct
  • Promo / commission / notes reviewed

After Approve

  • Confirm Warehouse Records appeared
  • Fill shipping + contact for PIC
  • Move status when ready for Warehouse
Done when: submission is Approved, Warehouse rows exist, and Part 1 details are filled for the PIC.
