HQ tab

Approve pending requests · hand off to Warehouse

For HQ / Approvers. Open a Pending Approval request, fill the form, then click Complete Step to approve — that sends the work to Warehouse. Only the Approver can complete the step.

Page layout

Approve

  • List of Approvals — pending and past requests
  • Click a Pending Approval item to open its form

Warehouse Submit

  • After approve, fill warehouse handoff details here
  • Qty / variation / shipping for Warehouse PIC

1 · Open a Pending Approval

  1. Open the HQ tab → Approve.
  2. In List of Approvals, find a request with status Pending Approval (orange).
  3. Click that request (e.g. Lee-Tiktok-28July). The detail form opens on the right.

2 · Fill the approval form

Complete the fields inside the open Pending Approval record before you finish the step.

  1. Review Selected Products (Link) — confirm the right products are linked.
  2. Add Approval Notes if useful (e.g. “Can proceed”).
  3. Set Approver — select yourself / the approving member (required).
  4. If rejecting: fill Reject Reason, then click Reject.
Tip: Don’t leave Selected Products or Approver empty. Fix links first if products are missing.

3 · Complete Step = Approve

  1. Only the Approver can click Complete Step.
  2. Click Complete Step when the form is ready.
  3. That means Approve:
    • Status moves from Pending Approval → Approved
    • Warehouse Processing rows are created (one per selected product)
    • The request is ready for Warehouse handoff
Done when: the list shows Approved (green), and the request can move to Warehouse Submit for PIC details.

Reject (if not approving)

  1. Open the Pending Approval request.
  2. Fill Reject Reason.
  3. Click Reject — do not use Complete Step.

Quick checklist

Before Complete Step

  • Pending Approval item opened
  • Selected Products checked
  • Approver selected
  • Notes filled if needed

After Complete Step

  • Status = Approved
  • Warehouse rows created
  • Use Warehouse Submit for shipping details
