HQ tab

Approve pending requests · hand off to Warehouse

For HQ / Approvers. Approve on the Approve tab, then fill product details on Warehouse Submit before the record goes to the Warehouse PIC.

Page layout

Approve

  • List of Approvals — pending and past requests
  • Click a Pending Approval item to open its form

Warehouse Submit

  • After approve — fill remaining details per product
  • Use Open in side peek to enter shipping / qty / PIC

1 · Open a Pending Approval

  1. Open the HQ tab → Approve.
  2. In List of Approvals, find a request with status Pending Approval (orange).
  3. Click that request (e.g. Lee-Tiktok-28July). The detail form opens on the right.

2 · Fill the approval form

Complete the fields inside the open Pending Approval record before you finish the step.

  1. Review Selected Products (Link) — confirm the right products are linked.
  2. Add Approval Notes if useful (e.g. “Can proceed”).
  3. Set Approver — select yourself / the approving member (required).
  4. If rejecting: fill Reject Reason, then click Reject.
Tip: Don’t leave Selected Products or Approver empty. Fix links first if products are missing.

3 · Complete Step = Approve

  1. Only the Approver can click Complete Step.
  2. Click Complete Step when the form is ready.
  3. That means Approve:
    • Status moves from Pending Approval → Approved
    • Warehouse Processing rows are created (one per selected product)
    • Each product starts at Pending HQ Fill
Next: go to Warehouse Submit and fill the remaining details before Warehouse PIC can take over.

4 · Warehouse Submit — fill product details

After approve, HQ must complete the handoff fields for each product before it goes to Warehouse PIC.

  1. Switch to the Warehouse Submit tab (same HQ page).
  2. Find rows with status Pending HQ Fill (orange). Use the status chips or filter if needed.
  3. Hover the record title and click Open in side peek (side panel icon).
  4. In the side peek, fill all required fields:
    • Phone Number
    • Shipping Address
    • Variation
    • Colour
    • Size
    • Qty
    • Warehouse PIC — select the warehouse member
  5. Click Complete Step when the form is ready.
  6. Repeat for each product row still at Pending HQ Fill.
Tip: Side peek keeps the list visible while you edit one product at a time. Don’t skip Warehouse PIC — that’s who picks up the job on the Warehouse tab.
Done when: status moves to Pending Warehouse and Warehouse PIC can see the record on the Warehouse tab.

Reject (if not approving)

  1. Open the Pending Approval request.
  2. Fill Reject Reason.
  3. Click Reject — do not use Complete Step.
  4. Related Warehouse Processing rows (if any were created after a prior Approve) are set to Ended automatically — they will not go to Warehouse PIC.
Note: Reject before Approve = no warehouse rows created. Reject after Approve = existing warehouse handoff lines are cancelled (Status → Ended).

Quick checklist

Approve tab

  • Pending Approval item opened
  • Selected Products checked
  • Approver selected
  • Complete Step clicked

Warehouse Submit tab

  • Pending HQ Fill rows found
  • Open in side peek → fill all fields
  • Warehouse PIC selected
  • Complete Step → Pending Warehouse
