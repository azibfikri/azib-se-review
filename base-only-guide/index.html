How to use this Lark Base App

One Base, three workspaces. Submit products and request approval on Products, decide on HQ, then fulfill on Warehouse. Status lives in Flow — Approve / Reject drives the rest.

1 · ProductsSubmit products & request approval
2 · HQReview list · Approve or Reject · fill warehouse details
3 · WarehouseUpdate affiliate, shipping & Lalamove tracking

Products Product Submission & Approval Request

For affiliate / e-com teammates who submit products and ask HQ to approve.

Product Submit

  1. Open Products.
  2. Use Product Submit to add a product.
  3. Fill product details (name, URL, price, image, etc.).
  4. Link the correct Creator (affiliate) — pick once; HQ & Warehouse will see it automatically.

Sent Approval / Approval Request

  1. After products are ready, create an Approval Request.
  2. Give a clear Submission Title (campaign / requester name).
  3. Link the products under Selected Products.
  4. Submit — HQ gets notified; status starts as Pending Approval.
Tip: One submission should map to one creator’s products when possible. Mixed creators show up as a multi-creator list for HQ.

HQ Approval list · Approve / Reject · Warehouse handoff

For HQ / approvers who decide yes or no, then prepare details for Warehouse PIC.

  1. Open HQ and use filters: Pending Approval, Approved, Rejected.
  2. Open a submission — check linked products, creator info, and notes.
  3. Decide:
    • Approve — Flow Status becomes Approved → Warehouse rows are created automatically.
    • Reject — Flow Status becomes Rejected → team is notified (add a reject reason when needed).
  4. After approve, fill HQ Part 1 details for Warehouse PIC (qty, variation, size/colour, shipping address, phone, etc.).
  5. Use warehouse status filters (Pending HQ Fill → Ready / Pending Warehouse → Done) to track handoff.
Remember: Approve is what creates Warehouse Processing rows. Do not skip linking products before approving.

Warehouse Affiliate details · Lalamove · Shipping

For Warehouse PIC who packs, ships, and updates tracking for affiliates.

  1. Open Warehouse — rows are grouped by creator / shipment work.
  2. Confirm product + creator on each line (already pulled from the approved submission).
  3. Update affiliate / fulfilment details as needed.
  4. Add Lalamove tracking link and shipping progress.
  5. Upload packaging photo if required, then move status forward (e.g. Shipped → Pending HQ Review → Done).
Tip: If creator looks wrong, fix it on the product (Creator link) — Warehouse creator fields follow from there.

Quick checklist

Before you Approve

  • Products linked on the submission
  • Creator looks correct
  • Notes / discount / promo reviewed

Before you mark Done

  • Address & phone filled
  • Lalamove link added
  • Packaging proof uploaded (if needed)
