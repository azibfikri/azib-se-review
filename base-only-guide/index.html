How to use this Lark Base App

One Base, three workspaces. Submit on Products, decide on HQ, fulfill on Warehouse. Not every role sees every tab — check your access below. Homepage is visible to all roles.

1 · ProductsSubmit products & request approval
2 · HQReview · Approve / Reject · handoff details
3 · WarehouseAffiliate, shipping & Lalamove tracking

Who can see which tabs?

App Advanced Permissions control your tabs. If a tab is missing for you, that is expected for your role — you are not blocked by a bug.

Role Homepage Products HQ Warehouse
Approver Can view Can view Can view Can view
HQ Can view Can view Can view No access
Warehouse Can view No access No access Can view

What each role does

Approver

Sees all tabs. Can work across Products, HQ, and Warehouse. Edits Product Selection, Product Approval, Warehouse Processing, and Creators.

HQ

Sees Homepage, Products, and HQ — not Warehouse. Focus on product + approval work. Can edit Product Selection, Product Approval, and Creators (not Warehouse Processing).

Warehouse

Sees Homepage and Warehouse only. Edits Warehouse Processing; Product Selection / Product Approval are view-only; Creators has no access.

Homepage (everyone)

Shared start page for every role. Use it to understand the flow and which tabs you should open next.

Tip: Open only the tabs your role shows. Approver can see everything; HQ stops before Warehouse; Warehouse starts at Homepage then goes straight to Warehouse.
