How to use this Lark Base App

One Base, three workspaces. Submit on Products, decide on HQ, fulfill on Warehouse. Use the tabs your account shows — Homepage is available to everyone.

1 · ProductsSubmit products & request approval
2 · HQReview · Approve / Reject · handoff details
3 · WarehouseAffiliate, shipping & Lalamove tracking
Note: Not everyone can see every tab. Access depends on your role. If you need a tab that is missing, please contact IT Admin.
