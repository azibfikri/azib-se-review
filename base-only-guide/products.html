Products tab

Product Submission & Approval Request

For affiliate / e-com teammates. Add products here, link the Creator once, then send an approval request to HQ.

Who uses this tab?

Anyone submitting products for affiliate campaigns and asking HQ to approve them.

You do here

  • Submit new products
  • Link the correct Creator
  • Create an Approval Request
  • Attach selected products to the request

You don’t do here

  • Approve / Reject (that’s HQ)
  • Fill shipping / Lalamove (that’s Warehouse)

A · Product Submit

  1. Open the Products tab in Base Only.
  2. Click Product Submit.
  3. Fill product details — name, URL/listing link, price, image, SKU / internal code, promo flags as needed.
  4. Set Creator to the affiliate (link field). Pick once — HQ and Warehouse will see the same creator automatically.
  5. Save. Your product appears in the Products list/gallery.
Tip: Don’t leave Creator blank. Wrong or empty creator means HQ can’t see who the affiliate is, and Warehouse grouping will be wrong.

B · Approval Request (Sent Approval)

  1. When products are ready, click Sent Approval / create an Approval Request.
  2. Write a clear Submission Title (campaign / requester name + platform + date works well).
  3. Link products under Selected Products.
  4. Add notes for the approver if needed.
  5. Submit. Status becomes Pending Approval and HQ is notified.
Done when: submission shows in HQ under Pending Approval, with your products linked.

Checklist before you send

Product

  • Image + listing URL filled
  • Creator linked
  • Price / commission correct

Approval request

  • Clear submission title
  • Correct products selected
  • Prefer one creator per submission
