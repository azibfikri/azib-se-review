Warehouse tab

Pack · ship · confirm delivery

For Warehouse PIC. Open Pending Warehouse rows in side peek, add packaging photo and Lalamove tracking, then use Complete Step to move forward. Only Warehouse PIC can click Complete Step.

Who uses this tab?

Warehouse teammates who pack, ship, and confirm when the parcel reaches the owner.

You do here

  • Open rows at Pending Warehouse
  • Upload packaging photo + Lalamove link
  • Complete Step → Shipped
  • Complete Step again when delivered → Done

Already filled for you

  • Submission link
  • Product link
  • Creator (from product)
  • Shipping address, phone, qty, variation (from HQ)

Status flow

1Pending Warehouse — pack & ship
2Shipped — Pending HQ Review
3Done — parcel arrived
Remember: Only Warehouse PIC can click Complete Step at each stage.

1 · Open Pending Warehouse in side peek

  1. Open the Warehouse tab. Records are grouped by creator (e.g. Aina Beauty, Rina Hairlab).
  2. Find a row with status Pending Warehouse (blue/orange).
  3. Hover the record and click Open in side peek.
  4. Confirm product + creator match what HQ approved.

2 · Pending Warehouse — pack & ship

Fill the required fields in the side peek, then complete the step.

  1. In the Pending Warehouse stage, fill:
    • Packaging Photo — upload proof of packed parcel
    • Lalamove Tracking Link — paste the booking / tracking URL
  2. Only Warehouse PIC clicks Complete Step.
  3. Status moves to Shipped — Pending HQ Review.
Done when: packaging photo and Lalamove link are saved, and status shows Shipped.

3 · Shipped — confirm delivery

When the parcel has arrived at the owner, close out the line.

  1. Open the same record in side peek (now at Shipped — Pending HQ Review).
  2. Confirm delivery with the owner / tracking.
  3. Only Warehouse PIC clicks Complete Step again.
  4. Status moves to Done — shipment successful.
Done when: status shows Done (green) and HQ can see the line is complete.

Quick checklist

Pending Warehouse

  • Side peek opened
  • Packaging photo uploaded
  • Lalamove link pasted
  • Warehouse PIC → Complete Step

After Shipped

  • Parcel confirmed arrived
  • Warehouse PIC → Complete Step again
  • Status = Done
