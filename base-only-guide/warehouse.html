Warehouse tab

Affiliate details · Lalamove · Shipping

For Warehouse PIC. After HQ Approves, update fulfilment details, tracking, and shipping progress for each affiliate line.

Who uses this tab?

Warehouse teammates who pack, ship, and update tracking for approved submissions.

You do here

  • Open rows created after Approve
  • Confirm product + creator
  • Update affiliate / shipping details
  • Add Lalamove tracking + packaging proof

Already filled for you

  • Submission link
  • Product link
  • Creator (from product)
  • PID / listing link (when fanned out)

How to process a shipment line

  1. Open the Warehouse tab. Rows are often grouped by creator.
  2. Confirm Record Title, product, and creator match what HQ approved.
  3. Complete / correct affiliate and fulfilment details from HQ Part 1 (address, phone, qty, variation).
  4. Add the Lalamove tracking link when the job is booked.
  5. Upload packaging photo if required.
  6. Move status forward (e.g. Pending Warehouse → Shipped — Pending HQ Review → Done).
Tip: If the creator is wrong, don’t just edit Warehouse text forever — fix the Creator on the product record so formulas stay correct.

Before you mark Done

Must have

  • Shipping address + phone
  • Lalamove tracking link
  • Correct creator / product

Nice to have

  • Packaging photo
  • Clear notes for HQ
  • Status updated so HQ can filter
Done when: tracking is live, packaging proof is attached if needed, and status shows the shipment is complete.
